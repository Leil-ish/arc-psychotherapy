# Site Writing Standards

## What To Prioritize

- recognitional accuracy
- behavioral specificity
- real costs and tradeoffs
- actual next moves
- useful clarity over polish

## What To Avoid

- therapist-template empathy
- broad praise of the reader
- conceptual density for its own sake
- branded inclusivity filler
- soft reassurance without traction

## Approved Patterns

- Name the loop.
- Name the cost.
- Name what people already know that has not changed anything.
- Name what the work is for.
- Name what the work is not for.

## Disallowed Patterns

Do not use:

- you may be
- you might be
- quietly carrying
- holding it together
- safe space
- healing journey
- hold space
- authentic self
- clarity and compassion
- meet you where you are
- gentle support
- deeply held
- inclusive space
- aligned care

## Sentence Test

Good site copy usually does one of these:

- identifies a pattern
- identifies a cost
- explains a useful distinction
- gives a concrete next step

If a sentence does none of those, it probably does not need to stay.
