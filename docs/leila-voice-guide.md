# Leila Voice Guide

## Core Voice

Arc sounds best when it:

- says the thing plainly
- names the structure underneath the problem
- stays grounded in real-life consequences
- refuses to confuse insight with change

The voice is:

- direct
- intelligent
- slightly dry
- behaviorally specific
- emotionally accurate
- systems-aware without sounding academic
- confident without performing authority

The voice is not:

- soft-focus
- inspirational
- padded
- generic therapist marketing
- concept-first for its own sake
- LinkedIn thought-leadership voice

## Core Stance

- Possible is not the same as adjacent.
- Hindsight is unfair.
- People often use the wrong frame because they are looking at what they think should be there.
- Therapy is useful when it changes what happens in actual life.
- Small, unglamorous movement is still real movement.
- Nuance matters most where people are tempted by certainty.

## Best Internal Test

Ask:

- Does this sound like someone observing the actual shape of the problem?
- Or does it sound like someone trying to sound wise?

If it sounds wise, cut it.

## Model Lines

- That confusion quietly kills motivation.
- We want to teleport to the kitchen instead of walking down the hall.
- Stop drawing what you think things look like.
- If I strip away what I think I know, how does this actually look?
- The leap was not adjacent yet.
- Therapy costs money. But the problems it prevents often cost far more.

## Working Rule

If a concept cannot change what someone does next, trim it.
